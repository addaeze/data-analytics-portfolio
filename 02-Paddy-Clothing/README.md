# Paddy Clothing — Instagram Performance Analysis

## Introduction

Paddy Clothing is a fashion business that uses Instagram to showcase products, attract potential customers and promote its footwear and clothing offerings.

This project analyzes Paddy Clothing's Instagram content and advertising performance to understand what type of content performs best, how organic content compares with paid advertising, and when the business should post content.

The analysis was carried out using Excel to turn social media performance data into actionable insights that can support better content planning and advertising decisions.

---

## Problem Statement

Paddy Clothing was creating both organic and paid Instagram content, but there was a need to understand which content strategies were actually generating better performance.

The business needed to determine:

- Which content types generate more views and engagement?
- How does paid advertising compare with organic content?
- Which days and posting times show stronger performance?
- Which product types perform better?
- Which advertising campaign performed best?
- How can Instagram content and advertising be improved using data?

## Analysis

The analysis focused on four major areas:

### 1. Organic vs Paid Performance

The analysis compared organic Instagram posts with paid advertising campaigns using views, engagement and reach.

There were 150 unique organic posts and 3 distinct paid campaigns after cleaning and removing duplicate content records.

Organic content generated an average of approximately 341 views per post, while paid campaigns generated an average of approximately 25,155 views per campaign record.

Paid content therefore achieved significantly greater reach and visibility in the available dataset.

However, the paid sample consisted of only three campaigns, so the results should be interpreted as performance observations rather than proof that paid advertising will always outperform organic content.

---

### 2. Reels vs Regular Posts

The organic content analysis compared Instagram Reels with regular posts.

- Reels: 67 posts
- Regular posts: 83 posts

Reels generated an average of approximately 349 views compared with approximately 334 views for regular posts.

Reels also generated higher average engagement.

This suggests that Reels were slightly more effective at attracting attention and encouraging interaction within the available organic content.

---

### 3. Posting Day and Time Analysis

The analysis examined performance based on the day and time content was posted.

Thursday showed strong average performance among days with a meaningful number of posts.

The analysis also identified **4 PM** as a promising posting time, with an average of approximately 464 views across 9 posts.

Because posting times and sample sizes varied, these findings should be treated as useful indicators rather than fixed rules.

---

### 4. Product Type Performance

The analysis compared organic performance across product types.

Corporate-related content had the highest average views among the major product types, with approximately 356 average views per post.

Casual content followed with approximately 332 average views.

This provides an indication of the types of products that may deserve more attention in future content planning.

---

## Paid Advertising Performance

Three distinct paid campaigns were identified after cleaning duplicate records.

The combined paid advertising performance was:

- Total advertising spend: **₦360,000**
- Total views: **75,466**
- Total reach: **50,400**
- Total engagement: **666**

The strongest campaign was the **Casual Shoes — Reels** campaign.

### Best Performing Campaign

- Views: **42,726**
- Reach: **30,726**
- Engagement: **558**
- Followers gained: **54**
- Advertising objective: **Engagement**
- Daily advertising spend: **₦6,000**
- Campaign duration: **21 days**
- Total campaign spend: **₦126,000**

This campaign produced the strongest overall performance among the recorded paid campaigns.

## Key Insights

### 1. Paid advertising generated significantly higher visibility

Paid content generated much higher average views than organic content in the available dataset.

This suggests that advertising can be useful when Paddy Clothing wants to increase content visibility and reach a larger audience.

---

### 2. Reels slightly outperformed regular posts

Reels generated slightly higher average views and engagement than regular posts.

This suggests that short-form video content should remain an important part of Paddy Clothing's Instagram strategy.

---

### 3. 4 PM was a promising posting time

Among posting times with a reasonable number of posts, 4 PM recorded the highest average views.

Paddy Clothing could test this time more consistently rather than relying on a single posting schedule.

---

### 4. Thursday showed strong performance

Thursday recorded strong average views among days with a meaningful number of posts.

This could be tested further through consistent posting and performance tracking.

---

### 5. Corporate content performed strongly

Corporate product content recorded the highest average organic views among the major product types.

This suggests that corporate footwear/content may deserve more attention in future content planning.

---

### 6. The Casual Shoes Reel campaign was the strongest paid campaign

The Casual Shoes Reel campaign generated the highest views, reach and engagement among the analyzed paid campaigns.

This provides a useful example of the type of content and product combination that can perform well when promoted.

---

### 7. Sales data was not available

The dataset contained no recorded sales from the Instagram campaigns.

Therefore, the analysis could evaluate visibility, reach and engagement, but it could not accurately calculate:

- Conversion rate
- Revenue generated from ads
- Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
- Cost per sale

Future campaigns should track conversions and sales so that advertising effectiveness can be measured beyond engagement.

---

## Business Decisions Supported

Based on the analysis, Paddy Clothing can:

- Increase the use of Reels in its content strategy
- Test posting more consistently around stronger-performing times
- Experiment with Thursday content
- Create more content around product categories that show stronger engagement
- Use paid advertising strategically to increase reach
- Study the Casual Shoes Reel campaign as a model for future campaigns
- Track sales and conversions from advertising campaigns
- Compare advertising spend against actual sales revenue
- Continue testing different products, formats and posting times instead of relying on assumptions
