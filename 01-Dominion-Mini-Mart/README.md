# Dominion Mini-Mart — Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Introduction

Dominion Mini-Mart is a retail business that sells everyday consumer and household products. The business experienced periods of declining sales and customer loss, creating a need to better understand its sales performance, product profitability and purchasing patterns.

I worked on a sales and profitability analysis to identify the products, categories and periods contributing most to revenue and profit. The analysis was designed to transform transactional sales data into actionable business insights that could support inventory planning, product prioritization and sales decisions.

The project covers sales transactions from May 2024 to January 2025.

## Problem Statement

Dominion Mini-Mart was experiencing challenges around declining sales and customer retention. Without a clear view of which products were generating revenue and profit, it would be difficult to determine which products should receive greater inventory priority and which periods or categories required attention.

The analysis therefore focused on answering:

> What products, categories and sales periods are driving Dominion Mini-Mart's revenue and profitability, and where are there opportunities for improvement?

## Project Objectives

- Analyse overall sales and profitability.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Identify the most profitable products.
- Compare category performance.
- Analyse monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Identify weak-performing periods.
- Understand the relationship between sales volume, revenue and profit.
- Provide recommendations for inventory and sales decisions.

## Key Insights

### December was the strongest month

December recorded the highest revenue of approximately ₦11.44M and the highest profit of approximately ₦2.05M.

### November was the weakest month

November recorded the lowest monthly revenue at approximately ₦6.35M.

### Rice generated the highest revenue

Rice 5kg generated approximately ₦5.71M in revenue.

### Milo generated the highest profit

Milo 500g generated approximately ₦864,740 in profit.

### Revenue was concentrated among top products

The top 10 revenue-generating products contributed approximately 45% of total revenue.

## Business Decisions Supported

The analysis could help management:

- Prioritise high-revenue products when planning inventory.
- Maintain adequate stock levels for profitable products.
- Prepare additional inventory ahead of strong seasonal periods.
- Investigate weak-performing months.
- Focus promotions on products that combine strong demand and profitability.
- Improve purchasing and inventory planning.
